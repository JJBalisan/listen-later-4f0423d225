vespers live artist/dj callout

Vespers live artist slash D J callout. An application form for artists.

The form asks you to sign in to Google to save your progress.

The questions

The form begins by asking for your artist name, and your email, which is required.

Next it asks where you are based.

It then asks you to provide a link to a recent recorded set, mix, or original production, on SoundCloud, YouTube, or Bandcamp.

After that, it asks for links to your social media channels, such as Instagram, Resident Advisor, or TikTok.

It then asks whether you are applying as a D J, a Hybrid Act, or a Live Electronic Act. This answer is required.

You are also asked to describe your sound in three genres or keywords.

The last question asks about your availability for the upcoming months.

At the end, there are two buttons: Submit, and Clear form.

The form carries a reminder never to submit passwords through Google Forms, and a note that the content is neither created nor endorsed by Google.
